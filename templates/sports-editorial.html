A controlled publishing pipeline

One workflow from research to release

CXMS keeps the human expertise that makes sports statistics valuable, while removing the repetitive formatting, tagging and validation work that slows editorial teams down.

01

Submit

Journalists enter curated bullet-point statistics using a clear, guided form.

02

Review

Sub-editors check wording, accuracy, duplication and readiness for publication.

03

Enrich

Athletes, countries, competitions and events are connected to approved entities and links.

04

Generate

CXMS validates the content and produces JSON that matches the required publishing schema.
